Frequently asked questions

No answer here? Ask on WhatsApp.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what the website needs to do. After a short chat I send a written proposal with price and timeline, before we start.

How long does it take?

A business website is quicker than an online shop or a custom system. The timeline is set in the proposal and you follow progress on a preview link.

Can I edit the text myself?

Yes. I can include a simple panel to change text, images and projects, and I will show you how it works.

Do you handle domain and hosting?

Yes. I help you choose and set up the domain, hosting and professional email, or I work with what you already have.

What happens after launch?

You can keep a monthly maintenance plan: updates, backups and small changes. Or you get everything handed over and explained, and run it yourself.

Do you only work in Portugal?

No. I am based in Santa Maria da Feira and work with clients anywhere, over video calls and WhatsApp, in Portuguese or English.
